# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Visitors encountering Shanzoon's work for the first time, and returning viewers looking for a fresh visual discovery.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Shanzoon through one clear Chinese sentence, its English echo, and one daily selected image; offer four quiet visual entrances into the existing archive under a statement that joins generative exploration with aesthetic judgment; then reveal how image-making becomes a working system before ending directly on the real Loomicc interface and contact. The category-first `trend-lab/<category>/YYYY-MM-DD` tree remains the factual image source without dictating the visitor's navigation; run metadata lives separately under `trend-lab/_runs`.

## Brand Personality

Nocturnal, collected, exact. The interface should feel like a carefully arranged black studio stage emerging from a quiet dotted darkness.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dated archive browser, a generic portfolio grid, four labeled uniform cards, ornamental glass panels, and any scroll effect that traps or delays the visitor.

## Design Principles

1. Make identity legible before navigation.
2. Let the artwork carry the color.
3. Use one short scroll sequence to turn introduction into discovery.
4. Keep the four entrances visually distinct and text-free on the home page.
5. Show real project evidence after the archive without turning the page into a conventional portfolio.
6. Keep private systems private when a safe public artifact does not exist.
7. Keep the archive as the single source of truth.
8. Make every interaction work with pointer, keyboard, and touch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus behavior. Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve keyboard navigation, provide image names as alternative text, and never encode category by color alone.
